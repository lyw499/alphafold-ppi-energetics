# Methods and scope

One target: MG_191–MG_192; no other Pedro pairs or full datasets recomputed.

Existing full-metric sources: pedro_full_bsa/partial/a063_j0007.csv; mgen_reference_bsa_comparison/tables/reference_interfaces.csv; Pedro contacts_a063_j0007.parquet; mgen_same_species_control/mgen_experimental_contact_geometry.parquet. The completed matching audit determines candidate identity and initial surface correspondence.

Sequence mapping: EV1 full sequences; experimental entity canonical sequences; Biopython global PairwiseAligner, match +2/mismatch −3/gap-open −5/gap-extend −0.5/free terminal gaps. Retain identical positions consistent across all optimal alignments (up to 128); map author residue identifiers through label_seq_id. Restrict further to residues observed on both chains. For 6YRK, do not force ambiguous repeated-sequence positions. All experimental interface contact residues pass stable mapping. This mapping uses sequence and coordinates present, never contact recovery to shift residue numbering.

Full contact counts and distances are reused. AF3 model SHA256, eligible sequence, all 1,437 saved contact minima, 151 close contacts and its saved BSA are validated on the single selected model. Each experimental saved contact minimum is reconciled with its matched coordinates. Exact corresponding AF3 distances beyond 8 Å are computed only for experimental contact pairs. No global all-atom distance matrix is stored. All atoms exclude H/D, first coordinate model and default alternate conformer choices match previous parsers.

Common-residue contacts are obtained by restricting both endpoints to the stable observed mapping. Common-residue BSA is recomputed on these trimmed chain pairs using the frozen Shrake–Rupley 100-point/1.4 Å probe implementation and (SASA_A+SASA_B−SASA_AB)/2. It is a restricted-coordinate surface area, not regression-adjusted BSA. Missing residues can affect intra-chain solvent occlusion; the full result is preserved alongside it. SASA_A/B follow each structure's own chain order; experimental A/B may be reversed relative to AF3 C/E. BSA and contact counts are invariant to that order.

Close-contact intervals: (3,4] Å. Transition intervals: ≤3, (3,4], (4,5], (5,6], (6,8], >8 Å. Leaving (3,4] does not always mean a lost ≤8 Å contact. Experimental-to-AF3 transition tables contain both retained and changed pairs; new AF3 close pairs are accounted for separately within common residues. Missing-heavy-atom diagnostics use the prior standard-residue backbone/side-chain atom-name dictionary. Common-atom distance control retains atom names present in both structures for each residue; it does not reconstruct unobserved atoms. Cα RMSDs use least-squares rigid-body fits with proper rotations on common positions; chain-wise and joint two-chain fits have different meanings.

No inferential p-values, bootstrap over copies, new QC removal, or assumption of independent PDB copies. Comparability tiers are explicit in interface_comparability.csv. 6RUT main copies are primary; additional corresponding structures and low-resolution/alternative surfaces are separately labelled. Pair identity alone is insufficient to establish interface equivalence. The model has multiple chains; only C/E enter interface metrics.

Run: scripts/run_compute.sh in persistent tmux pedro_mgen_case_20261026; logs/compute.out and compute.err; checkpoints/status.json and per-interface checkpoints. Only the single known inner ZIP is temporarily unpacked to recover the one chosen CIF; it is removed after extraction. Existing results are never overwritten.
