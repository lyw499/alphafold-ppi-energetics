# Methods and assumptions

Primary observation: an interchain residue pair, minimum non-H/D atom distance ≤8 Å; close means 3 < d_min ≤4 Å. Existing code uses searchsorted(..., side="left") at [3,4,5,6,8]; boundary checks passed. No interval change. Pedro count_3_4 is reused; benchmark counts independently tallied from saved residue minima and reconciled with saved fractions. No CIF/ZIP reads.

Analysis units: saved interface observations. Full matched FoldBench 132 interfaces preserve the original pooled comparison; 147 unavailable AF3 cases are not zero. Strict 109-interface mapping is only required for paired physical/identity controls, which are separate. SAFES 50 reliable chain pairs are not necessarily 50 curated binding partners. Zero-contact counts are 0; their fractions are NA. Both zero-inclusive and nonzero-8Å analyses are reported. Count-zero among nonzero-8Å interfaces is retained. QC removes only the existing Pedro <0.5 Å severe-overlap flag; other flags are retained, not converted into exclusions.

Unpaired effect sizes: Pedro−reference difference of interface-weighted medians (inverse empirical CDF quantiles), Cliff's delta=P(Pedro count>reference count)−P(Pedro count<reference count), difference of interface-weighted mean log(1+count), and descriptive KS distance D. Ties contribute zero to Cliff's delta. No iid-contact or naive iid-interface KS p-value is reported. Median CIs can be discrete; a median contrast does not test all aspects of a distribution.

Inference: 2000 independent cluster-bootstrap resamples per dataset, seed 20261024. Experimental clusters are PDB IDs, containing all assemblies/pairs. Pedro clusters are original job names, merging archive records with the same name. Within sampled clusters all interfaces are retained, and resulting interface histograms are normalized; the estimand remains interface-weighted, not equal-job-weighted. Percentile 95% CIs for median contrast, Cliff's delta and log-mean contrast. The test is an approximate cluster-robust Welch t-test of equal interface mean log(1+close count): per-group variance G/(G−1) × sum_cluster[sum_i(y_i−mean)]² / N²; independent-group variances added; Welch–Satterthwaite df. This tests a location contrast on a specified transformed count scale, not equality of every feature of the two distributions. exp(log-mean difference) is the ratio of geometric means of 1+count, NOT a raw mean-count ratio.

Four primary contrasts = 2 references × zero-inclusive/nonzero-only, raw Pedro/job clustering. Holm correction across these four. QC and coarse-archive sensitivity families get their own Holm corrections and are exploratory, not additional confirmatory tests. 95% effect-size intervals are marginal, not simultaneous. Coarse clusters merge all archives connected by repeated job names. Archives are computational batches, not established independent biological samples. Proteins recur across jobs and batches; this crossed dependence is not fully solved by either grouping. Significance is therefore conditional/exploratory, not a definitive biological population test. No causal effect or accuracy difference follows from unpaired nonrandom datasets.

Repeated exact source/chain keys are checked. Repeated protein pairs across predictions are preserved and audited; they are not declared independent protein pairs. Possible same-job/model/chain representations across archives receive a separate first-representation descriptive sensitivity; matching metadata/counts cannot prove coordinate identity. A per-protein-pair median table is also saved as a descriptive sensitivity, not iid inference.

Count-size matching: among Pedro nonzero interfaces, nearest log(1+total 8 Å contacts), all exact ties weighted equally at interface level. Each experimental nonzero interface gets one Pedro conditional mean close count. This new count contrast is descriptive and does not use old fraction contrasts as substitutes. Matching reused Pedro observations is not treated as independent paired data. Total contact count is an endogenous geometric proxy, NOT buried area in Å².

Area: existing ΔSASA/2 is available for strict benchmark pairs and only a selected Pedro pilot. Pilot values are marked in the standardized table; no full-Pedro area-adjusted test, no imputation from contact count, and no area-driven causal interpretation. The paired-area figure is contextual, not a fitted adjustment. A valid future area-adjusted analysis needs representative Pedro area coverage and a stated model/sample design.

Pooled fraction=sum(close counts)/sum(total contacts), weights large interfaces more; mean/median interface fraction weights interfaces; count distribution retains absolute interface scale and zeros. Earlier total-contact matching addresses a third conditional estimand. Selection, size, protein identity, repetitions, zero inflation and quality all limit interpretation.
